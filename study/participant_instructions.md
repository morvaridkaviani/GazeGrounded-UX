# Instructions for participants

Thank you for taking part! The study takes about 15 minutes.

**Before you start**

1. Use a laptop or desktop computer with a webcam, in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Sit about an arm's length from the screen. Make sure your face is evenly lit and there is
   no bright window behind you.
3. Close other camera apps (video calls, etc.).
4. Have your **participant code** ready (for example `P07`). Never enter your name or e-mail.

**During the study**

* The page switches to full screen. Please keep it that way.
* **Calibration:** red dots appear. Look at each dot and click it 5 times *while looking at
  it*. Then just look at the blue dots — no clicking.
* **Tasks:** read the task, open the interface, then click where you would do it. The pictures
  are not real websites; nothing happens when you click except recording the click. If you
  cannot find it, press **“I can't find it”**. There are no right or wrong answers — we are
  testing the designs, not you.
* After each task, rate how easy it was and, if you like, say why.
* Try to keep your head roughly still.

**At the end**

Click **Download data (JSON)** and send the file to the researcher as agreed. Then close the tab;
the camera switches off. If you change your mind, just close the tab without downloading.
