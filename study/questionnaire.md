# Questionnaire

## After each task (in the browser study)

1. **Single Ease Question (SEQ)** — “Overall, how easy or difficult was this task?”
   7-point scale, 1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy (Sauro & Dumas, 2009).
2. **Open question** — “What made this task easy or difficult?” (optional, max. 500
   characters; participants are reminded not to enter personal information).

## After the session (optional, on paper or a separate form — not built into the prototype)

| # | Question | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Age group | 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64, 65+, prefer not to say |
| 2 | How often do you shop / bank online? | never … daily (5-point) |
| 3 | Do you wear glasses or contact lenses during the study? | glasses / contacts / neither |
| 4 | Did anything disturb you (noise, lighting, interruptions)? | free text |
| 5 | Did the eye-tracking calibration feel accurate? | 5-point |

Collect only what the analysis needs (data minimisation). Demographics are reported in
aggregate only.

## Coding feedback (researchers)

Code each comment into one category: findability, visual_hierarchy, ambiguous_wording,
information_overload, feedback_uncertainty, navigation, no_friction, other. Two coders,
blind to condition; report Cohen's kappa; resolve disagreements by discussion. Store as
`data/raw/study/labels.csv` (`trial_id, friction_label, feedback_category`).
