Interface study

Thank you for considering taking part. Please read this before you start.

  • The study takes about 15 minutes: a short calibration, then 9 short tasks on pictures of websites.
  • Your webcam is used to estimate where you look on the screen. Estimation runs only inside this browser tab.
  • No video or images of you are recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Only estimated screen coordinates, clicks, timings and your answers are kept.
  • Do not enter your name, e-mail or other personal details anywhere in the study.
  • You can stop at any time by closing this tab; nothing is saved until you choose to download your data at the end.

Participant code

Enter the code you received from the researcher (e.g. P07). Do not use your name or e-mail.

2–12 letters, digits, - or _.

Set-up

  1. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, face evenly lit, no strong light behind you.
  2. Keep your head still-ish and avoid leaning during the study.
  3. The study will switch to full screen. Please keep it in full screen.

Click start; your browser will ask for camera access.

Look at each dot and click it 5 times while looking at it.

Calibration check

Click on the place in the interface where you would do this. If you cannot find it, use “I can't find it”.
